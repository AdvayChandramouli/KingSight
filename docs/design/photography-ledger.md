# Photography ledger

Cleared plates for the Relief landing (Direction B). Geography only: these photographs are context for King County sales recorded May 2014–May 2015. They are not listing photos and not evidence of that market year.

Access date for every row: **2026-11-12**.

No plate is a placeholder. No image was generated. No color grade was baked into the files. The hero original is already overcast water, concrete, and a gray sky, so a later CSS grade can move it toward cedar, concrete, and water without fighting a teal cast.

## Selection

The hero is the overcast Elliott Bay frame rather than the clear-sky cruise panorama also on Commons (`File:Seattle skyline seen from Elliott Bay.jpg`, CC0, Ron Clausen, 2017). That second frame is a bright blue travel view. It was not downloaded into the app. The fabric plate is a street of houses, not a second copy of the skyline.
